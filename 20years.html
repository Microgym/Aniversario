Our journey

From a small idea to a company with an international outlook.

Rinfresco was founded on 29 September 2006. Over the years, we have expanded our product categories, markets and business relationships while staying true to a simple idea: work with professionalism, closeness and continuity.

Today we trade in perfumery, food, household and cleaning products and, of course, beverages — different categories that reflect how Rinfresco has evolved and diversified over the past 20 years.
